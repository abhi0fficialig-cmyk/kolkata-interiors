About Kolkata Interior

We design homes the way your family actually lives.

Kolkata Interior & Furniture is an interior design company based in Kolkata, West Bengal. We handle complete home interiors – modular kitchens, living rooms and TV units, wardrobes and storage, false ceilings and lighting, custom woodwork, home renovation and luxury interiors – so the planning, the design, the execution and the final finish all stay with one team.

Since we design and execute it ourselves, the choices are yours. Pick the layout that suits your space, finishes that can handle kids and chai spills, colours that sit well with the way you live. We plan it, execute it, hand it over and stand behind it. Modern, stylish and made to last – your dream home, our promise.

FAQs

Questions we get asked a lot

Can you design interiors to suit my space and budget?

Yes. Every project is designed around your home, so you choose the layout, materials, colours and finishes. Share your floor plan, room measurements or a few photos and we will guide you.

How long does an interior project take?

It depends on the size of the project and the materials. We share a clear timeline before work begins, so you know the handover date upfront.

Do you handle the execution as well?

Yes. Our own team handles the work on site across Kolkata – carpentry, ceilings, lighting and finishing – and leaves the space clean at handover.

Can you renovate my existing home?

Yes. We can redo kitchens, wardrobes, ceilings, lighting and wall finishes so an old space looks and feels new again. Send us a photo for a quick estimate on WhatsApp.
